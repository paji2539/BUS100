Objective 12.3

The environment marketing operates in

No marketing plan exists in a vacuum — forces inside and outside the company shape what works.

Internal environment
tap to flip
Factors within the company itself — resources, culture, other departments’ decisions.
External environment
tap to flip
Factors outside the company’s control that still shape its strategy.

External factors to watch

👥
Demographic
Age, income, education, and other population characteristics.
📈
Economic
Inflation, employment, and overall spending power.
💻
Technological
New tools and platforms that change how customers shop.
🏛
Political
Regulations and government policy affecting the industry.
🌐
Cultural
Shifting values, trends, and social norms.
Objective 12.4

Researching before you launch

Good marketing starts with good information — guessing is expensive.

Market research
Collecting and analyzing information to guide marketing decisions.

Two types of data

Primary data
tap to flip
Information collected firsthand for a specific purpose — surveys, interviews, experiments.
Secondary data
tap to flip
Information that already exists, collected by someone else for another purpose — industry reports, government data.

What a marketing plan includes

  • Objectives
  • Situational analysis
  • Target market
  • Marketing mix
