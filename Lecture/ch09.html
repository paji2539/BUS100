Objective 9.2

Training, developing, evaluating

Hiring someone is the beginning of HRM’s job, not the end.

Training methods▼
  • Orientation
  • On-the-job training
  • Apprenticeships
  • Virtual training
  • Simulation training
Management development▼
  • Job rotation
  • Coaching
  • Mentoring
  • Action learning
Performance evaluation▼
  • Performance appraisals
  • Goal setting
  • Feedback
  • 360-degree appraisal
360-degree appraisal
A performance review that gathers feedback from a manager, peers, and sometimes subordinates or customers — not just the boss.
Real-world example
Many tech companies now run continuous, ongoing feedback instead of a single annual review — treating performance evaluation as a habit, not an event.
Objective 9.3

Pay, schedules, and the employee lifecycle

Compensation is more than a paycheck, and every employee eventually leaves — the question is how.

What "getting paid" can include

💵
Compensation
Salaries, wages, commissions, and bonuses.
🏦
Benefits
Health insurance, retirement plans, 401(k), profit-sharing, and paid time off.
🕑
Flexible work options
Telecommuting, flextime, compressed workweeks, and job sharing.

How employment ends

Tap each card — separation isn’t always a bad thing.

Promotion
tap to flip
Moving an employee up to a role with more responsibility — a form of separation from their old position.
Termination
tap to flip
Ending an employee’s job, either for cause or as part of a layoff.
Retirement
tap to flip
An employee voluntarily leaving the workforce, often after years of service.
Buyout
tap to flip
A company offers an employee compensation to voluntarily leave, often to reduce headcount without layoffs.
Objective 9.4

Diversity strengthens the workforce

A workforce that reflects a range of backgrounds tends to make better decisions — but getting there isn’t automatic.

Benefits of diversity▼
  • More perspectives on any given problem
  • Better problem solving
  • Improved global understanding
  • Greater innovation
Challenges▼
  • Cultural differences
  • Generational differences
  • Communication barriers
Diversity training
Programs designed to promote inclusion and improve relationships across a diverse workplace.
In-class discussion
Think of a time diverse perspectives improved a group project, team, or class decision. What made the difference?

Objective 9.5

Labor unions and collective voice

When individual employees feel they have little bargaining power alone, some organize together.

Labor union
An organization that protects workers’ interests, typically through collective negotiation with employers.

What unions push for

  • Better wages
  • Improved working conditions
  • Job security
Collective bargaining
Negotiation between a union and an employer over wages, hours, and working conditions.

How unions apply pressure

Strikes
tap to flip
Employees collectively stop working until demands are met.
Picketing
tap to flip
Public demonstration outside a workplace to draw attention to a dispute.
Boycotts
tap to flip
Encouraging the public to stop buying from a company until it meets union demands.
Discussion question
Should more industries be unionized today? What would workers gain — and what might companies argue they’d lose?
