Objective 10.3

Business goes social and mobile

Social platforms and phones didn’t just change how people talk — they changed how businesses market, hire, and sell.

What businesses use social media for

📢
Marketing
Reaching customers directly, often for a fraction of traditional ad costs.
💬
Customer service
Answering questions and resolving complaints in public view.
👤
Recruiting
Finding and vetting job candidates.
⭐
Brand awareness
Building visibility and reputation over time.

Two ways the crowd gets involved

Crowdsourcing
tap to flip
Getting ideas, content, or work from a large group of people, often online.
Crowdfunding
tap to flip
Raising small amounts of money from a large number of people, usually online.
Social media monitoring
Tracking mentions and conversations about a brand across social platforms.

Mobile technology

  • Mobile apps
  • Mobile commerce
  • Cloud computing

Two terms people often confuse

Machine learning
tap to flip
A type of AI that learns patterns from data and improves its performance over time, without being explicitly reprogrammed.
Generative AI
tap to flip
AI that creates new text, images, code, or other content, rather than just analyzing existing data.

What’s coming next

  • Quantum computing
  • Increasingly capable AI-powered business tools
