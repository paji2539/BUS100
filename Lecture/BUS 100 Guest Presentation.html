Silicon Valley Bank: gone in 48 hours

  • 2019–2021Assets grow from $71 billion to over $211 billion
  • 2022The Fed raises interest rates quickly; SVB's bonds lose value
  • March 8SVB sells bonds at a $1.8 billion loss and announces it needs more capital
  • March 9Depositors withdraw about $42 billion in one day
  • March 10Regulators close the bank
  • March 12The government guarantees all deposits
  • March 2023First Citizens buys $72 billion of SVB assets for $16 billion
  • Fall 2026SVB name retired: now First Citizens Innovation Banking and Fund Banking

Finance: where did the money go?

  • Over 55% of SVB's assets were in its investment portfolio by the end of 2022.
  • When rates rose, those bonds fell in value: interest rate risk.
  • To pay depositors, SVB had to sell bonds at a loss: liquidity risk.

Risk management: who was watching the risk?

8 months
without a chief risk officer, April 2022 to January 2023, while rates rose fastest
  • Late 2021: regulators flag weak liquidity risk management.
  • May 2022: findings on ineffective board oversight and risk management.
  • Deposit insurance is risk transfer. 94% of deposits had none.

Five things to do while you're here

  • Learn to communicate. Writing, speaking, and professional email.
  • Get experience before senior year. Jobs, clubs, projects, and internships all count.
  • Build relationships. Professors and professionals who know your name.
  • Use AI, but check it. Ask good questions and spot wrong answers.
  • Be dependable. Do what you said, when you said you would.

When no one checked the AI

2023, New York: Lawyers were sanctioned after filing a brief citing court cases ChatGPT made up.

2024, Canada: Air Canada was held responsible for wrong refund information its chatbot gave a customer.

AI mistakes are now a business risk that someone has to manage.
