What is a Business?

A business offers goods, services, or both with the goal of earning a profit.

What businesses did you use in the last 24 hours?

Profit vs Nonprofit

Profit = Revenue - Expenses

Nonprofits generate revenue but reinvest it into their mission.

Business Environment

Competition

Better products, lower prices, branding, customer satisfaction.

Social Environment

Aging population, diversity, green movement.

Globalization

Offshoring, new markets, competition, political risk.

Technology

E-commerce, AI, cybersecurity, privacy.
